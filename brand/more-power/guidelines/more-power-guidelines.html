MORE POWER · Logo guidelines

Version 1.0 · How to use the logo, its colours and its files. Keep this guide with the logo files.

1. The idea

Each O in the name becomes a power switch. MORE is navy with a blue switch; POWER is blue with a navy switch. Together they express two complementary forces, real assets and power capacity, working as one system. In the standalone mark, the two switches are coupled like circuits and meet in one shared core.

Two words. Two power switches. One connected system.

MORE represents the assets and the platform. POWER represents the capacity they unlock. The reciprocal colours connect the two, and the coupled mark expresses cooperation, transfer and shared momentum.

Rule: on dark backgrounds every navy element reverses to off-white. Every blue element becomes electric blue.

Small sizes

For small digital use on light backgrounds, prefer the navy one-colour version whenever maximum contrast is needed (below 128 px wide, or in text-heavy interfaces). Deep blue on white has a contrast ratio of about 3.3:1. It is meant for the standard-size wordmark and display use. Electric blue on navy (about 6.9:1) is strong at every size.

One-colour versions

Black, white and navy (Pantone 289 C) versions are for single-colour printing and for embossing, engraving, laser cutting, embroidery, vinyl cutting and other one-colour production methods. For embroidery and cutting use the simplified files in print/production/. Don't make two-colour versions in other colours.

4. Clear space

X equals one half of the outer diameter of the power-button O in the primary wordmark. Measure clear space from the outermost edge of the artwork, including the tip of each switch, not from the text baseline or an internal counter. The stacked logo uses the same X. For the mark, X is one quarter of a ring's outer diameter. Nothing, including text, other logos or the edge of the page, goes inside the clear space.

6. Typography

  • Source of the wordmark: Inter Display, version 4.000 (git a52131595), SemiBold, weight 600. Inter Display is the static display optical size of Inter.
  • Spacing: tracking +35/1000 em, word space 0.24 em, the font's own OpenType kerning, and Os optically fitted by hand.
  • The Os are custom geometry: power-button rings matched to the font's overshoot and stroke contrast. They are not glyphs from the font.
  • Production files use outlined artwork and don't need the font. Never retype the logo.
  • Brand typefaces: Inter Display (SemiBold or Bold) for headlines; Inter (Regular and Medium) for text. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, free for commercial use. Fallback: system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif.

7. Motion

The supplied switch-on animation plays once and holds. Do not loop it by default. When the viewer has asked for reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion), show the static logo instead. Don't animate the logo in any other way.

8. Don'ts

  • Don't rebuild the Os from a stock power icon, or type the name in another font.
  • Don't swap the colours of the two words, or of the switches.
  • Don't separate a switch from its O.
  • Don't place the mark inside an unapproved circle, shield or badge.
  • Don't use the coupled mark as an O inside the wordmark.
  • Don't add "Fund" or any other descriptor directly to the artwork.
  • Don't use the expressive shared-O lockup below its minimum size, or for signage and official documents.
  • Don't put the name inside the app icon or avatar: they use the mark alone.

10. Trademark

The trademark has not been searched yet. Power-button symbols are a crowded category, so run a professional trademark search before registering the logo or printing it at scale. The search should cover:

  • MORE POWER as a word mark;
  • the complete wordmark with its power-button Os;
  • the coupled two-O mark;
  • the energy, investment, real-estate and infrastructure classes.
