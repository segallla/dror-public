MORE POWER · Logo guidelines

How to use the logo, its colours and its files. Keep this page next to the logo files.

1. The idea

Each O in the name is a power switch. MORE is navy with a blue switch; POWER is blue with a navy switch. The mark puts the same two Os back to back, overlapping like a transformer.

2. Versions

Use the primary logo wherever it fits. The other versions are for the formats named under each one.

Primary · headers, documents, signage
Primary reversed · dark backgrounds
Secondary · square formats, entrance signs
Mark · app icon, avatar, patterns
Mark reversed
Single O · favicon, 16–24 px only

3. Clear space

Keep a free margin of X on every side, where X is half the height of the O. Nothing else, including text, other logos or the edge of the page, goes inside it.

4. Minimum size

Below these sizes the switches stop reading. Switch to the smaller version listed instead.

5. Colour

Use one blue per background. On light backgrounds use Deep blue, which has enough contrast for small sizes. On navy or other dark backgrounds use Electric blue. CMYK and Pantone values are close matches; check them against a printed swatch book before the first print run.

One-colour versions, in black, white or navy, are for single-colour printing, embossing, engraving and fax. Don't make two-colour versions in other colours.

6. Typography

  • Logo: Inter Display SemiBold, with custom Os. It is already outlined, so you never need to type the logo.
  • Headlines: Inter Display, SemiBold or Bold. Body: Inter, Regular and Medium.
  • Licence: SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use. Fallback: system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif.

7. Don'ts

  • Don't rebuild the Os from a stock power icon, or type the name in another font.
  • Don't swap the colours of the two words, or of the switches.
  • Don't separate the switch from its O, or animate the logo other than with the supplied switch-on animation.
