# MORE POWER logo kit — release notes

## 1.0 — 19 October 2026

First release.

- Primary wordmark: MORE in navy with a blue switch, POWER in blue with a navy switch. Os rebuilt as
  power-button rings matched to Inter Display's overshoot and contrast; spacing set with the font's kerning,
  +35/1000 em tracking and hand-fitted Os.
- Top opening refined: narrower cut and a switch that ends at the ring line, so each O stays a closed, clear O.
- Logo hierarchy: primary, primary reversed, stacked (new), mark, expressive shared-O (campaign use only),
  single O.
- Mark: two identical rings, overlap reduced by 13 %, meeting in one protected core with a wider clear gap.
  Below 48 px the core is dropped (small cut); below 24 px the single O is used.
- Colour: reversed mapping table and rule; navy one-colour recommended for small digital use on light
  backgrounds.
- Clear space defined from the outer diameter of the power O and shown on primary, stacked and mark;
  minimum sizes given as minimum widths.
- Motion: switch-on animation in CSS only; plays once and holds, never loops; honours reduced motion.
- Print: CMYK and Pantone spot-colour vector PDFs, one-colour PDFs, CMYK EPS, and simplified production
  artwork for embroidery, engraving and cutting.
- App icons: iOS, Android (adaptive and legacy), Play Store, social avatars — mark only.
- Guidelines as HTML and PDF.

Known limits:
- The print PDFs are not certified PDF/X-4 (no embedded output-intent profile); the printer should convert.
- The trademark has not been searched.
