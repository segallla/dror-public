What we heard

The brief

An investment fund that acquires industrial and logistics assets with large existing grid connections. The identity should feel disciplined, durable and confident, with one clear idea about power that investors remember.

disciplineddurableconfidentenergetic

Success: one idea, readable as a name at any size, works in one colour and reversed, ownable beyond the generic power icon.

MORE POWER — final identity2
Final design

MORE POWER

Each O in the name is a power switch: MORE is navy with a blue switch, POWER is blue with a navy switch. In the mark, the two switches are coupled and meet in one shared core.

  • Two words, two power switches, one connected system
  • The mark is the two Os of the name, coupled like circuits
  • Inter Display with custom Os; outlined artwork, no live type
